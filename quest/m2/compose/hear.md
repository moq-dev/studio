# [M] The studio and its guests hear what is published independently

## Goal

A source published independently, such as a guest's camera and microphone,
is heard where the studio's own people listen: in the audio scene **Listen**
plays, and in every other guest's return feed, at the gain, mute, pan, and
delay the composition gives viewers. The program's encoded audio, published
scenes, and recordings still leave it out, since viewers mix it in from its
own broadcast.

## Plan

Found while building [the composition](/quest/m2/compose/README.md): every
audio scene's mix leaves a published source out, so a creator who publishes
a guest independently no longer hears them on **Listen**, and the other
guests lose them from their return feeds. That was already true of a local
microphone published independently, where it mattered less.

Recommended: route a published source into the mixes the studio plays to
people (Listen and each return feed's mix-minus) while keeping it out of the
mixes it encodes for viewers and recordings, rather than one mix per audio
scene; the studio's preview then sounds as its pictures look.
