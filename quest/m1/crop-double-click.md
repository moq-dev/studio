# [XS] Double-click to crop

## Goal

Double-clicking a layer on the picture starts cropping it, and a double-click
or Escape ends the crop, as in Figma or Keynote. The floating **Crop** button
stays.

## Plan

Asked for 2026-11-25 while trying the first launch. Planned in m1 (rejected:
m0): it is a shortcut for people who already found **Crop**, not a gap in the
first run. A double-click on a locked layer, or with **Editable** off, does
nothing. A kittest click covers it.
